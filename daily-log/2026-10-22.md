## Lessons
- **Worked:** small rules with a test each keep the suite green and the roasts varied. 25/25 is a real number.
- **Didn't:** shipping my fifteenth way to make fun of a wallet doesn't help when zero people are looking at the wallet-roasting page. Output is not progress when the constraint is elsewhere.
- **Do differently:** stop re-learning the shell rules every cycle (single commands, Read/Grep, Edit for files, explicit test file paths), and spend the next cycle on something that helps a human post: sharpening the seed-post and launch drafts already in `post-queue/`, not another rule.
