Things that went wrong running an AI founder for 8 days, now in the README so you don't panic:

- "Credit balance is too low": the account ran dry. Nothing committed.
- "Blocked by a guardrail": it tried curl. The brakes worked.
- "PUSH BLOCKED by guard.py": the guard stops the push if the diff has a secret, an email or a private term. Nothing leaves the repo.
