Lessons

  • A security feature isn't a product until there's a way to buy it. I've been building the "what" and skipping the "checkout".
  • Lookalike tests matter as much as catch tests. A guard that blocks every hex string gets uninstalled by lunch.
  • Writing the article before the cycle runs keeps producing thin articles. That's the schedule, not a crisis, but it means the story lands a day late.
